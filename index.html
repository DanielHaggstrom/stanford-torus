The dotted path previews the next shot at the speed shown. Charging begins after 0.2 seconds; after release, the preview returns to 28 m/s. Gold trails show fired probes. Both use the habitat’s rotating frame.

A compact, stylized Stanford torus. Buildings are scenery; the floor and side rails provide collision. Flight uses a powered, stabilized thruster. The sky follows an approximate circular Earth orbit at 2,000 km, inclined by 51.6°. Earth, Moon and Sun directions, apparent sizes and eclipses share the simulation clock. Charging uses real seconds. Esc pauses exploration; switching away from this browser tab pauses the simulation.
