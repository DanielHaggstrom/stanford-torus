The dotted path previews the next shot at the speed shown. Charging begins after 0.2 seconds; after release, the preview returns to 28 m/s. Gold trails show fired probes. Paths follow your current reference frame: curved with the ring inside, straight in inertial space. In space, future contact accounts for the surface rotating before the probe arrives.

A compact, stylized Stanford torus. Buildings, trees, and solid props block movement and probes. Flight keeps a deck-relative horizon inside and a steady orientation in space, with automatic braking. The sky follows an approximate circular Earth orbit at 2,000 km, inclined by 51.6°. Earth, Moon and Sun directions, apparent sizes and eclipses share the simulation clock. Charging uses real seconds. Esc pauses exploration; switching away from this browser tab pauses the simulation. Sound begins after your first input and cuts out beyond the pressure enclosure. Players can pass through its roof and sides; probes stop on the shell. In orbit view, the separate central axis tracks the Sun with tilting solar/radiator wings and holds its pose during eclipse.
